# Animation demo models

From the Khronos glTF Sample Assets
(https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets), imported into
`Content/Animation` with the editor's glTF importer (skeletons, skinned meshes
and clips). Their textures are left out; LightLab tints them with its PBR
material.
